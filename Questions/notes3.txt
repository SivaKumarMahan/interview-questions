 Terraform’s state file is your lifeline. Mess with infra by hand? Expect drift, failed destroys, and team headaches.Practice: Import a live resource into Terraform, tweak it wrong, run a plan, and spot the diff.

2. CI/CD Pipelines
 Build-test-deploy seems simple until shared runners jam everything. Q: How do you keep one bad pipeline from halting the show?Practice: Set up GitHub Actions with matrix builds, caching, and concurrency limits.

3. Secrets Management
 Never stash secrets in Git even private repos. K8s Secrets? Just base64, not encrypted easy pickings for anyone with access.Practice: Use KMS for envelope encryption, integrate Vault or SOPS, and rotate keys on the fly. Ditch static AWS creds.

4. Kubernetes Networking
 Can’t tell ClusterIP from NodePort? Time to level up. Q: Pod A ghosts Pod B how do you troubleshoot?Practice: Deploy a pod, fire up tcpdump/traceroute, and kubectl exec in for a look.

5. Observability
 Logs narrate, metrics trend, traces reveal the real deal. No cardinality checks? Hello, Prometheus meltdown.Practice: Spin up Prometheus + Grafana, add custom metrics to an app, break it, and trigger an alert.

6. Scaling & Auto-Healing
 “It autoscales” until your pod loops crashing forever.Practice: Set HPA on CPU/mem, blast load with hey, and watch the scale-up magic (or fails).

7. Networking & Load Balancing
 NGINX, Traefik, ALB—pick your poison, but health checks can lie and black out half your setup.Practice: Tune readiness vs. liveness probes, botch one on purpose, and see the outage hit.

8. Disaster Recovery
 Backups are worthless without tested restores. Q: How do you DR drill in prod without chaos?Practice: Snapshot an RDS, nuke some data, restore to a clone, and check integrity.

9. Immutable Infrastructure
 No more snowflake servers patch ’em live and regret it.Practice: Bake AMIs via Packer, deploy with Terraform, and nuke/rebuild daily.

10. Container Security
 Root in Docker feels safe… until it bites.Practice: Scan with Trivy, patch CVEs, and switch to non-root users in your Dockerfile.
